## 2. Narrative Summary and Analysis
During the reporting period from January 01, 1970, to January 01, 1970, a total of 1,024 safety cases were received. Among these, 1,023 cases were classified as serious and 1 case was classified as non-serious. The most commonly reported reactions during this period were acute kidney injury (22 cases), drug ineffective (12 cases), cerebral haemorrhage (7 cases), hyponatraemia (6 cases), and cholestasis (6 cases).

## 4. Reaction / Adverse Event Analysis
An analysis of the reported adverse reactions indicates that the most frequent reactions overall are acute kidney injury (22 cases), drug ineffective (12 cases), cerebral haemorrhage (7 cases), hyponatraemia (6 cases), and cholestasis (6 cases). The distribution of the most frequent reactions in serious cases is identical, with acute kidney injury reported in 22 cases, drug ineffective in 12 cases, cerebral haemorrhage in 7 cases, hyponatraemia in 6 cases, and cholestasis in 6 cases.

The distribution of reported outcomes shows that the most frequent single outcome is "recovered/resolved" with 147 entries, followed by "recovering/resolving" with 82 entries, "not recovered/not resolved/ongoing" with 67 entries, and "unknown" with 66 entries. Multiple-outcome combinations were also frequently reported, including "recovered/resolved,recovered/resolved" (76 entries), "recovered/resolved,recovered/resolved,recovered/resolved" (52 entries), "unknown,unknown" (35 entries), "recovering/resolving,recovering/resolving" (29 entries), and "recovered/resolved,recovered/resolved,recovered/resolved,recovered/resolved" (29 entries). Fatal outcomes were also documented in the dataset, including 18 entries of "fatal" and 18 entries of "fatal,fatal".

System Organ Class (SOC) level analysis is unavailable in this dataset.
